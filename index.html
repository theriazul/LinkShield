Security learning

Learn how to spot scams

Phishing messages often create urgency and mimic legitimate services. A single trusted verification channel can reduce risk.

Check the full hostname, look for misspellings, odd subdomains, and insecure HTTP. When in doubt, verify through official channels.

Attackers often use similar-looking domains, punycode, or added hyphens to imitate trusted brands. Compare the exact host carefully.

Use long passphrases with unique combinations, avoid reuse, and enable multifactor authentication on critical accounts.

QR codes can hide links to fake login pages or payment requests. Inspect the destination before opening it, and verify with the source.
